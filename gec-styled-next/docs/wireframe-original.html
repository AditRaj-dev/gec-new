WHO WE ARE

We Don’t Just Talk About Entrepreneurship.
We Create Space to Experience It.

Galgotias Entrepreneurship Cell is a student-driven community built around innovation, leadership, creativity, and entrepreneurship. Through mentorship, workshops, startup-focused programs, and collaborative experiences, GEC encourages students to turn curiosity into action.

ORIGIN & EVOLUTION

From Curiosity to Community.

GEC exists to bring together students who want to think differently, solve problems, and explore entrepreneurship beyond classrooms.

The community grows around startup development, pitching sessions, workshops, founder interactions, networking, and practical experiences that allow students to learn entrepreneurship by participating in it.

GEC works within the wider Galgotias innovation ecosystem alongside the Galgotias Incubation Centre for Research, Innovation, Startup & Entrepreneurs — GICRISE.

GICRISE supports startups and student innovators through mentorship, exposure, networking, incubation, and growth opportunities.

INCUBATION PIPELINE MATRIX

Student Idea to Market Venture

STAGE 01 · GEC COMMUNITY
Ideation, team formation, hackathons & peer builder cohorts
STAGE 02 · SDP ACCELERATOR
12-week intensive prototyping, pitch coaching, and MVP validation
STAGE 03 · GICRISE INCUBATION
Formal seed grant funding, legal incorporation, and angel demo day
OUR MISSION

Create Builders, Not Spectators.

Our mission is to foster an entrepreneurial mindset among students by creating opportunities to ideate, collaborate, experiment, lead, and execute.

We aim to connect students with the knowledge, mentorship, and ecosystem required to move from curiosity toward meaningful action.

OUR VISION

A Campus Where Ideas Have Somewhere to Go.

Our vision is to build a thriving student entrepreneurship ecosystem where ambitious ideas can find collaborators, guidance, opportunities, and a pathway toward becoming impactful ventures.

Transforming campus talent into fearless problem solvers ready to lead India’s technology and economic frontier.

FLAGSHIP PROGRAM: COHORT 04

STARTUP DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (SDP)

A 12-week intensive pre-incubation sandbox for Galgotias student builders, providing mentorship, pitch coaching, and fast-track GICRISE incubation.

Route: /initiatives/sdp-cohort-04
● 12-Week Curriculum & Venture Milestones
PHASE 01
Weeks 1–3: Ideation
Problem validation, TAM sizing, and 50+ customer interviews.
PHASE 02
Weeks 4–6: MVP Build
Prototyping sandbox, tech architecture, and no-code MVPs.
PHASE 03
Weeks 7–9: Traction
Campus pilot testing, initial user feedback, and metric tracking.
PHASE 04
Weeks 10–12: Demo Day
Pitch deck polishing and closed-door presentations to angel VCs.
OVERVIEW & WHY IT EXISTS

SDP bridges the gap between classroom theory and real venture creation. Built by student founders for student founders, it equips participants with customer discovery frameworks, legal basics, and functional prototyping guidance.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
✓ Galgotias Student Builders & Aspiring Founders
✓ Multidisciplinary Teams of 2 to 5 Members
✓ Working Proof of Concept or Validated Problem Statement
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Who is eligible to apply for SDP Cohort 04? +
Any registered student team of 2 to 5 members from Galgotias University or GCET with an original idea or working hardware/software prototype may apply.
What incubation support does GICRISE offer? +
Top cohort teams receive co-working desk access, legal incorporation assistance, patent filing facilitation, and closed-door demo presentations to angel investors.
Is there any equity or registration fee? +
Zero fees and zero equity. GEC is completely student-driven and supported by Galgotias University and GICRISE to empower student builders.
