Mobile Route Layouts

The phone version of Route Layouts: every route at 390×844, with the shell, the acts, and the shaders. Each frame is the live page — scroll it, tap it. On a real phone, open this page and pick a route. Blue tags mark sections for review; toggle them and the shaders with the buttons bottom-left.

Shell56px bar that hides on scroll-down · menu and Dispatch as native bottom sheets · Dispatch pill (Apply pill on Initiatives) that steps aside for the footer, forms and the stories aisle.
ActsDesk → initiatives bookshelf · Stories aisle kept as-is · Stage Manager → sticky team chips + swipe track (?team= deep links). Curtains → act headers.
Shaders on phonesSame four families (contour · halftone · hatch · riso). 0.35×DPR capped at 480px, 24fps, only on-screen hosts draw, max 4 live contexts. Hero mask moves to the top-right corner.
Shader fallbacksStill frame on reduced motion, Save-Data or <4GB RAM, and after 30 slow frames (>42ms avg). Any GL failure → flat CSS surface.
EntranceHome only, once per session: lockup at 70vw, 4s hold, flies into the bar logo, 700ms curtain split. Skip pill top-right. Replay link in the footer.
