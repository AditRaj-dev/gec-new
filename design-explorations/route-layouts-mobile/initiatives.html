Startup Development Program

12 weeks of mentorship, pitch coaching, and fast-track GICRISE incubation. Zero fees, zero equity.

  1. Phase 01 · Weeks 1–3

    Ideation

    Problem validation, TAM sizing, 50+ customer interviews.

  2. Phase 02 · Weeks 4–6

    MVP Build

    Prototyping sandbox, tech architecture, no-code MVPs.

  3. Phase 03 · Weeks 7–9

    Traction

    Campus pilots, first user feedback, metric tracking.

  4. Phase 04 · Weeks 10–12

    Demo Day

    Deck polish and closed-door pitches to angel VCs.

Eligibility

✓ Galgotias student builders & aspiring founders
✓ Teams of 2 to 5 members
✓ Proof of concept or validated problem statement

Frequently asked

Who is eligible for SDP Cohort 04?

Any registered student team of 2–5 from Galgotias University or GCET with an original idea or working prototype.

What incubation support does GICRISE offer?

Co-working desks, incorporation help, patent filing, and closed-door demos to angel investors.

Is there any equity or registration fee?

Zero fees and zero equity.
