From Curiosity to Community.

GEC brings together students who want to think differently, solve problems, and explore entrepreneurship beyond classrooms.

It grows around startup development, pitching sessions, workshops, founder interactions, and practical experiences.

GEC works alongside GICRISE — the Galgotias Incubation Centre for Research, Innovation, Startup & Entrepreneurs.
