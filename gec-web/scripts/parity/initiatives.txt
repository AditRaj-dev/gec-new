FLAGSHIP PROGRAM · COHORT 04
Startup Development Program (SDP)
A 12-week intensive pre-incubation sandbox for Galgotias student builders, providing mentorship, pitch coaching, and fast-track GICRISE incubation.
Phase 01 · Weeks 1–3
Ideation
Problem validation, TAM sizing, and 50+ customer interviews.
Phase 02 · Weeks 4–6
MVP Build
Prototyping sandbox, tech architecture, and no-code MVPs.
Phase 03 · Weeks 7–9
Traction
Campus pilot testing, initial user feedback, and metric tracking.
Phase 04 · Weeks 10–12
Demo Day
Pitch deck polishing and closed-door presentations to angel VCs.
Overview &amp; why it exists
SDP bridges the gap between classroom theory and real venture creation. Built by student founders for student founders, it equips participants with customer discovery frameworks, legal basics, and functional prototyping guidance.
Frequently asked questions
Who is eligible to apply for SDP Cohort 04?
Any registered student team of 2 to 5 members from Galgotias University or GCET with an original idea or working hardware/software prototype may apply.
What incubation support does GICRISE offer?
Top cohort teams receive co-working desk access, legal incorporation assistance, patent filing facilitation, and closed-door demo presentations to angel investors.
Is there any equity or registration fee?
Zero fees and zero equity. GEC is completely student-driven and supported by Galgotias University and GICRISE to empower student builders.
Eligibility criteria
✓ Galgotias Student Builders &amp; Aspiring Founders
✓ Multidisciplinary Teams of 2 to 5 Members
✓ Working Proof of Concept or Validated Problem Statement

IDEATHON 2026 · 48-HOUR VENTURE SPRINT
Pick a Problem. Build for 48 Hours.
Teams of 3–4 pick one statement, prototype it, and defend it in front of an angel panel.
Register your team →
PS-01 · AI
Lecture-to-Notes for Regional Languages
Turn a recorded Hindi or mixed-language lecture into searchable, structured notes a first-year student can revise from.
PS-02 · AI
Placement Prep Copilot
Match a student’s resume against campus recruiter JDs and generate a 14-day, gap-first preparation plan.
PS-03 · Climate
Campus Energy Leak Map
Use existing meter or IoT data to spot hostel blocks and labs that waste power, and nudge the people who can fix it.
PS-04 · Climate
Mess Food-Waste Loop
Predict daily mess demand and route surplus to NGOs or compost before it spoils.
PS-05 · Hardware
Sub-₹2,000 Air-Quality Node
A buildable sensor node that reports PM2.5 for Greater Noida classrooms and survives a monsoon.
PS-06 · Fintech
Student Credit Without a Credit Score
Design a fair way to underwrite small student loans (laptops, fees) using signals students already have.
