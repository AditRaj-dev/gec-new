# 3. Home

## 3.1 Hero

Purpose:

- Establish the GEC identity immediately
- Introduce the entrepreneurial ecosystem
- Communicate energy and action
- Direct visitors into the website

Primary actions:

- Explore Initiatives
- Discover GEC

---

## 3.2 What's Happening

Dynamic section for:

- Upcoming events
- Open applications
- Latest story
- Startup spotlight
- Current announcements

This section should be CMS-controlled.

---

## 3.3 Initiatives

Preview the major current and flagship initiatives.

Each card links to the relevant initiative page.

---

## 3.4 Impact

Show verified metrics only.

Possible metrics:

- Events
- Students engaged
- Startups supported
- Speakers and mentors

No unverified numbers should be published.

---

## 3.5 Spotlight

Purpose:

- Highlight major past moments
- Show event history
- Surface milestone experiences
- Build credibility through visual proof

Photo-led presentation preferred.

---

## 3.6 Stories

Preview:

- Founder Stories
- Startup Stories
- News
- Event Stories

---

## 3.7 Speakers

Show selected past speakers.

Use short cards with:

- Name
- Role
- Organisation
- Photo

---

## 3.8 Partners

Show relevant ecosystem, industry, community, and institutional partners.

---

## 3.9 Final CTA

Close the homepage with a clear action:

- Explore GEC
- Explore Initiatives
- Get Involved

# 7. Initiatives

# 10. Frozen Website Principle

The five top-level sections are frozen:

```text
Home
About
Teams
Initiatives
Stories
```

Any future functionality should first be placed inside one of these sections rather than creating unnecessary top-level navigation items.
