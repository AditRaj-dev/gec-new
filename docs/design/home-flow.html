  2. All three curtains stay. Each one wipes, then its act expands to full screen. Crimson share is about 35%, above the 25% target. That was chosen knowingly.
  3. The shelf holds founder and startup stories. Newsletter dispatches live only on /stories. The shelf curtain now reads “Every story that started here.” with an N STORIES count. That line is new copy, not from the frozen doc, so confirm or replace it.
  4. Teams sits on sand, matching the wireframe's /teams stage.
  5. The three full-screen components come from gec-showcase, not the gec-styled-next copies now sitting in gec-web. The showcase versions leave out the stray DeskFolio write-up, use the newer Stage Manager (wireframe role tags, already sticky at 100vh) and the original /stories newsletter section. Only two small hooks carry over: the data-gec-shelf-row attribute that the scroll-driven shelf travel reads, and the shelf's docked-shadow fix. The lib/ data files are identical in both projects.
  6. The wireframe's figures ship as written (12,000+, ₹50L, FarmVision AI and the rest), not [XX]+. This overrides the frozen copy's “verified data only” rule, so they should be checked before launch.
