# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Galgotias University students exploring entrepreneurship, aspiring student founders, builders looking for collaborators or support, and mentors or ecosystem partners evaluating GEC. They use the site to understand what is happening, discover initiatives and opportunities, and see credible evidence of an active student-led ecosystem.

## Product Purpose

Present Galgotias Entrepreneurship Cell as the public identity of an active entrepreneurship ecosystem rather than a college-club brochure. The experience should turn curiosity into participation by making current initiatives, stories, events, and pathways into the community clear and compelling.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, ambitious, and student-built. The voice is direct, youthful, entrepreneurial, and editorial without becoming bureaucratic, overly corporate, or slang-heavy.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic corporate university site, a purple-gradient startup template, a neon cyberpunk experience, or a glassmorphism-heavy SaaS site. Avoid generic SaaS cards, unverified claims, stock-led polish that hides the real community, and interactions that feel like decorative technology demos rather than purposeful brand moments.

## Design Principles

1. Show an ecosystem in motion through real work, people, initiatives, and current activity.
2. Make building feel tangible: use interaction and imagery to turn abstract entrepreneurship into things visitors can explore.
3. Modernize GEC heritage without replacing its established crimson, cream, gold, and blue identity.
4. Prioritize one clear story or action at a time; energy should strengthen hierarchy, not create noise.
5. Earn trust with verified facts, accessible content, and visible evidence instead of inflated marketing language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard access, semantics, and readable text. Essential information must remain available as real text, never only inside animation or video. Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve visible focus states, and keep all interactive experiences operable without precision pointing or motion.
